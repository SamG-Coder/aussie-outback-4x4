# Wombat Creek: a square kilometre of Victorian foothill bush, with a four-wheel-drive track
# looping through it, over two fords, up both sides' hills and through the bog flats.
# Make the truck's model first (tools/blender/prepare_raptor.py). Everything else is built
# from the `outback` line: the land, the creek, the track, and what grows.
texture_size 1024
camera -150 74 -150 0.4 -0.1
outback 1000 +crossroads
actor outback/raptor.actor -158.09 70.73 -160.11 177.1 shared
# The driver, who sits in the truck until they get out of it, and what the keys do.
actor outback/driver.actor -158.09 70.73 -160.11 177.1
actor outback/detector.actor -158.09 70.73 -160.11 177.1 shared
input outback/game.input
