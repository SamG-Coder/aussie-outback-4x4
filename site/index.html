What is new

The first alpha, and the first with its source open: the game and its engine are on GitHub under the MIT licence. The browser version and the Windows download have all of this; the Android app is free driving for now.

A living to make

A story with three kept games. An old truck, a block of land you owe for, and a metal detector: dig gold beside the tracks, carry it home in the tray, and sell it by weight in Ironbark Flat.

Fifty trucks

Each built of its own pieces, with bull bars, lifts, tyres, trays and snorkels that change how it drives.

A used-truck yard

Ironbark Motors, across the road from the town. Six trucks a day, each with its price on a board in front, and a different six tomorrow. Walk up and look one over.

Buying on a bill

Parts, paint and repairs go in a cart. The bill shows what it does to your debt, your Friday repayment and your interest before you say yes.

Damage by force

Every blow is measured in kilonewtons and each piece has its own strength. Pieces scuff, dent, hang loose and come off, and a bull bar takes the hit for the lamps behind it.

Damage that matters

Broken lamps go out, bent running gear pulls the steering, and a stove-in grille costs you power. It stays on the truck until Motor Spares mends it.

Mud that ruts

Tyres press ruts that stay, and the wheels behind ride in them. A spinning wheel digs itself down. Mud shines wet, cracks dry at its edges, and pools brown water.

Mud that flies

Clods and spatter leave the tyre the way the tread is going, and mark the ground where they land.

Your buttons on the screen

Hints follow what is in your hands: keys and mouse, Xbox or PlayStation, swapped the moment you change.

Where you drive

Three tracks and the road between

You start at The Crossroads, a dirt main road through the high country. Each track has a trailhead on it: drive into the ring, choose the time of day and the weather, and take it as free roam or timed laps.

Wombat Creek Track

A washed-out fire trail through stringybark and manna gum: two creek fords, bog holes, rock steps and ruts cut by a wet winter.

What you drive with

A truck that works like one

Transfer case

2H, 4H and 4L. Low range multiplies the engine's pull for climbs, bog holes and rock steps.

Diff locks

Rear, then front and rear. With open diffs a lifted wheel spins away your drive; locked, the others keep pulling.

Tyre pressure

Air down to float over mud and sand, and back up for the hard stuff.

Hill descent

Holds the truck back on steep drops, a wheel at a time, so you can stay off the brake.

Ground that gives

Clay, mud, bog, leaf litter, gravel, rock and water each grip, sink and spray differently, and rain changes all of them.

Day, dusk and night

Pick the hour and the weather for each track. After dark the high beams come on, and the tail lamps light under braking.

The bush

Manna gum, stringybark, ironbark, wattle, tree ferns, grass trees and bracken, each planted where it would grow.

Keyboard or controller

Keyboard and mouse, Xbox-style pads, and PlayStation controllers by USB or Bluetooth.

Play in your browser Download for Windows Download for Android

To run the download: unzip it anywhere and open AussieOutback4x4.exe. The first time, it fetches its maps before it starts, and its loading screen says how far along that is. Windows may ask before running a program it has not seen before; choose More info, then Run anyway.

On Android: open the file once it has downloaded. The phone asks whether the browser or the files app may install apps; allow it, and install. It is not in a shop, so the phone may warn about it first. The game is driven with the controls on the screen, held on its side, or with a controller, which hides them.
