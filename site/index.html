Victorian High Country

AussieOutback4x4

A four-wheel-drive game in the Australian bush. Low range, diff locks and aired-down tyres; red clay, bog holes, rock steps and creek crossings; and nobody else out here.

Play in your browser Edge or Chrome on a desktop PC. Streams as you drive, and keeps what it has downloaded. Download for Windows Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. About 40 MB, then 1.3 GB of maps the first time you play.

Pre-alpha 0.1  ·  free  ·  no account, no ads

Where you drive

Three tracks and the road between

You start at The Crossroads, a dirt main road through the high country. Each track has a trailhead on it: drive into the ring, choose the time of day and the weather, and take it as free roam or timed laps.

Wombat Creek Track

A washed-out fire trail through stringybark and manna gum: two creek fords, bog holes, rock steps and ruts cut by a wet winter.

What you drive with

A truck that works like one

Transfer case

2H, 4H and 4L. Low range multiplies the engine's pull for climbs, bog holes and rock steps.

Diff locks

Rear, then front and rear. With open diffs a lifted wheel spins away your drive; locked, the others keep pulling.

Tyre pressure

Air down to float over mud and sand, and back up for the hard stuff.

Hill descent

Holds the truck back on steep drops, a wheel at a time, so you can stay off the brake.

Ground that gives

Clay, mud, bog, leaf litter, gravel, rock and water each grip, sink and spray differently, and rain changes all of them.

Day, dusk and night

Pick the hour and the weather for each track. After dark the high beams come on, and the tail lamps light under braking.

The bush

Manna gum, stringybark, ironbark, wattle, tree ferns, grass trees and bracken, each planted where it would grow.

Keyboard or controller

Keyboard and mouse, Xbox-style pads, and PlayStation controllers by USB or Bluetooth.
