Aussie Outback 4x4 (pre-alpha)
https://rust4x4.com

The first time it is opened it downloads the game's maps into the app's folder (about 1.3 GB
to download, 1.9 GB on the phone), which needs the internet; after that only the pieces of a
map that have changed are fetched. Drive with the controls on the screen, or with a controller.
