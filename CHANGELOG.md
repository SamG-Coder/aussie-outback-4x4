# What is new

## Since Alpha 0.2

On the site from 16 November 2026, in the browser version, the Windows download and the
Android app. Not a tagged release.

- **Monster trucks.** In a free drive any of the fifty trucks can be stood on a monster
  truck's frame: a tube chassis, planetary axles on four links and twin shocks, and tyres
  1.68 m high. The truck keeps its own body, its own chassis and whatever is bolted to it.
  The axles, links, shocks and driveshafts move with the wheels.
- **A free drive you set up.** Choose any truck, build it from its own kit and paint it,
  make it a monster truck or not, then choose the map to start on, the time of day and the
  weather.
- **Bulldust Bowl.** A showground in red outback country, for a free drive: two mud pits,
  five jumps from a hop to a big-air ramp over a row of wrecks, and a steel loop.
- **Free drive: damage off, and start again.** Damage can be switched off for a free drive,
  before it starts or from the pause menu, and the pause menu starts its map again.
- **The Android app has the trucks.** Its download now carries the fifty truck packs, as the
  Windows one does. It has still not been run on a real phone.

## Alpha 0.2

The first alpha. The game is now open source under the MIT licence:
<https://github.com/SamG-Coder/aussie-outback-4x4>. The browser version and the Windows
download have the whole game; the Android app is still free driving only.

### A living to make

- **A story mode.** Three kept games. You start with an old truck, a block of land you owe
  for, and a metal detector. There is gold in the ground beside every track, somewhere
  different each time; dig it, carry it home in the tray, and sell it by weight.
- **Ironbark Flat.** A one-street gold town down the main road from your shed: a gold buyer,
  Motor Spares, a bank and a hotel. Every door is walked up to on foot.
- **Fifty trucks.** Each is built of its own pieces, with a kit of parts that change how it
  drives: bull bars, lifts, tyres, trays, snorkels and more.
- **Ironbark Motors.** A used-truck yard across the road from the town. Six trucks are parked
  there each day, each in its own colour with a price board in front, and the pool changes
  every day. Walk up to one to see its tag and look it over. New trucks are sold there too.
- **Buying on a bill.** Parts, resprays, oil changes and repairs go in a cart. The bill says
  what comes out of your cash, what goes on the debt, the new Friday repayment and the
  week's interest, and you can turn it down. A part is shown close up as you choose it.
- **Debt that grows.** Interest is added each Friday before the repayment comes out.
- **Timed runs** through gates on every track, with medals.
- **Sleep** to the morning, midday, afternoon, evening or night, whichever are six hours off
  or more.

### Damage

- **Piece by piece, by how hard it was hit.** The force of every blow is measured by the
  physics, in kilonewtons, and each piece of the truck has its own strength. Pieces are
  scuffed, dented, hang loose, and come off.
- **What is in front takes it first.** A bull bar spares the lamps behind it. A post hurts
  only what it meets. A hard landing comes up through the wheel that took it.
- **It changes the drive.** Broken lamps go out, bent running gear pulls the steering, and a
  stove-in grille costs the engine its pull.
- **It stays until it is mended.** Damage is kept with the truck, from map to map and in the
  kept game, and is repaired at Motor Spares.
- **A crash-test pad** (`crash.bat` in the source): seven lanes, from a wall to a rollover.

### Mud

- **Ruts that stay.** Tyres press ruts into soft ground, with berms beside them, and the
  wheels behind ride in the ruts of the wheels ahead. A spinning wheel digs itself down.
- **Mud that looks wet.** Dark and shining at its heart, drying to a cracked crust at its
  rim. Tread prints, brown water pooling in deep ruts, and wet tracks carried onto dry clay.
- **Mud that flies.** Clods, ropes and spatter leave the tyre the way the tread is going,
  and land as marks on the ground.

### Controls

- **The right buttons on the screen.** Hints follow what is in your hands: keys and mouse,
  Xbox buttons or PlayStation buttons, and they swap the moment you pick up another device.
- **Controller look speed** is an option.

### In the browser

- **The whole game.** The story, the town, the fifty trucks and the used-truck yard now play
  in the browser as they do on Windows. Each truck is fetched as it is needed, a few
  megabytes each.
- **Three kept games**, in the browser's own storage on the computer they were played on.

### Known rough edges

- None of the new damage, mud or yard has had much play yet. Expect numbers to be retuned.
- Fallen pieces are drawn but not solid. There is no crash sound, and paint does not scratch.
- The Android app does not have the story, the trucks or the town.
- In the browser a kept game lives in that browser on that computer: clearing the site's
  data deletes it, and it does not follow you to another machine.
- Medal times on the timed runs are not tuned.

## Pre-alpha 0.1

The first public build: The Crossroads and three tracks (Wombat Creek, Granite Ridge,
Blackwood Gully), low range, diff locks and tyre pressures, weather and time of day, sound,
and versions for the browser, Windows and Android.
