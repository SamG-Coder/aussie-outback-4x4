# ---- The engine: a supercharged V8, held at steady revs from an idle to hard at work. The game
# plays the two nearest the revs it is at, each sped up or slowed to match, and fades between
# them (apps/viewer/src/sound.rs). Each must be steady: a loop that revs up and down is no use.
#
# A loop comes out at whatever revs it comes out at, whatever its words ask for, and the game
# has to know the revs heard: ENGINE_LAYERS in sound.rs has them, as measured by
# engine_pitch.py. These four were chosen from several takes for a clear, steady note. After
# making any of them again with another seed or other words, measure it and set its revs.
